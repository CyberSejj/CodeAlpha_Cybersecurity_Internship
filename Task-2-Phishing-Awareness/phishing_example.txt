CODEALPHA PHISHING AWARENESS - TRAINING EXAMPLE

Subject: Urgent: Your Account Will Be Suspended

From: security-alert@example-training.com

Dear User,

We detected unusual activity on your account.

Your account will be suspended unless you verify your
information immediately.

Please verify your account using the link below:

[TRAINING LINK - DO NOT CLICK]

Thank you,
Account Security Team


PHISHING WARNING SIGNS:

1. The message creates urgency and fear.
2. The sender address is not an official company address.
3. It asks the recipient to verify account information.
4. The message contains a suspicious link.
5. The recipient should independently visit the official
   website instead of using the provided link.

SAFE ACTION:

Do not click the link.
Do not enter your password or OTP.
Verify the request through the organization's official
website or known contact method.
Report the message as phishing.
